Tally

I invest through DEGIRO, and DEGIRO shows you what you own and not much else. No graph of your portfolio over time, no overview of when you got dividend and how much of it, no yield on cost. So I built that part myself.

You feed it your DEGIRO CSV exports and it works out the rest:

  • portfolio value over time
  • dividend history, per position and total
  • projected dividends and yield on cost
  • daily price sync, everything converted to EUR

Laravel · Filament · PostgreSQL · Docker

More details on GitHub, or have a look at tally.d-jordy.nl if you have a DEGIRO account of your own. A demo account is on the todo list.
